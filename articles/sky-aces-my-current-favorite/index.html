Sky Aces is my current favorite

By Liam · 7 November 2026

Sky Aces is my favorite game on PlayPile right now. The first thing I like about it is how cheerful it looks for a game where somebody is almost always trying to shoot you down. The pilot wears goggles and a red scarf that flaps behind his open cockpit. Down below there’s a little harbor, a lighthouse and a whole lot of water to make a bad decision over.

The first mission gives you three rookies over Gull Bay. That sounds generous until you spend half the fight staring at one plane’s tail while the other two have a clear shot at yours. The little lead marker makes a big difference. Aim where the plane’s about to be, fire a short burst and give the guns time to cool. It’s satisfying when a shot lands because you read the turn right.

My favorite part is what happens when a chase falls apart. Climbing too hard can eat through your speed. A dive gets it back and gives you room to turn around. I like that a lousy approach can turn into a decent second pass if you spot the mistake early enough. And if you’re waiting on a missile lock, there’s always that tempting moment when you want to fire about a second too soon.

The campaign gets weirder in a good way. You go from those first few raiders to the Red Baroness, a bomber raid on the harbor and eventually the Leviathan airship. There’s a jet in the hangar if you keep at it. I have a soft spot for the starting Sparrow. Something about that scarf makes it very hard to root against the little guy.

If you’re trying it for the first time, start with Sky Aces’ First Flight mission. Give yourself one flight to get the feel for turning and another to see what happens when you let a plane go and come around for a second pass. That’s usually when “one more flight” starts sounding like a perfectly reasonable plan.
