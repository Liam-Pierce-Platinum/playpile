BLADE DASH with a story: smash in through the windows of a fifteen-storey castle, dash-slash your way up floor by floor and beat the rival ninja on the roof to save the princess.

CRAZY CATTLE

A knitted-sheep battle royale on a quilt in the sky. Ram the flock off the edge while the quilt unravels, and be the last sheep standing.

GETAWAY FLOP

A wobbly 1930s cartoon heist race. Hop across the rooftops to the getaway car, grab popguns and cream pies, and knock the other crooks off.

A wobbly rubber-hose heist race drawn like a 1930s cartoon, with bendy limbs, ink hatching, film grain and an iris wipe. Four crooks hop across the city rooftops to reach the getaway car, picking up popguns, blunderbusses and cream pies to knock each other off the roofs. MIRROR DUEL

One controller, two fighters. Your opponent copies every move you make, flipped. Win by making the same move hurt them more.

You control both fighters. Your cyan fighter gets your input, and the magenta MIRROR gets exactly the same input flipped left and right, so in an even room every punch would trade. Each of the eight neon arenas is lopsided on purpose: spikes on one side, a live electric wall, a conveyor belt, pits of different widths, lasers firing out of step, black ice, spiked ceilings.

SUMO SLAM

Hold to charge, let go to dash. Slam the other blobs off a shrinking, tilting ring and be the last one standing.

Four glossy sumo blobs on a ring floating in the sky, and one rule: be the last one on it. Hold to charge a dash, let go to launch, and a dash that connects throws the other blob hard. One that hits nothing can carry you straight off the edge. From 4 seconds the edge crumbles and the ring shrinks, from 6 seconds it tilts and keeps swinging round so everyone slides downhill, and at 48 seconds it starts collapsing fast, so no round reaches a minute. First to three rounds wins the match.

LANTERNFALL

Carry the last lantern through a haunted forest. Anything that steps into your light burns. Last five minutes until dawn.

You only move: everything fires on its own. Out in the dark the forest is pairs of glowing eyes, and anything that wanders into your lantern's circle slows down, catches fire and drops an ember. Collect embers to level up and pick one of three gifts - twenty-three in all, from orbiting wisps and firefly bolts to thorn hedges, shield moths and a sweeping lighthouse beam. Max a weapon, own its partner charm and open a chest, and it evolves into one of nine bigger forms.

TWISTER.IO

Be a tornado in a toy town: suck up cones, then cars, then houses, and be the biggest storm when the clock runs out.

Every round is two minutes in a bright toy town against nine rival storms, and the biggest one at the end wins. Steer over anything your size or smaller and it rips loose, spirals up and joins the ring of debris circling you. You start on cones and bins and climb through seven categories: people, cars and cows, trees and vans, houses, towers and the lighthouse, and finally the stadium.

SNOWBALL ROYALE

A snowball fight at dusk: duck behind snow walls, lob over them, and roll a giant one to flatten everybody.

Up to six kids, one snowy garden, ninety seconds. A click throws a fast flat snowball that a wall will stop; hold it and you lob one over the wall instead, and a red ring on the ground shows you where theirs are coming down. Scoop fresh snow to reload and you dig the ground bare, so nobody can camp. Build your own walls, belly-dive under throws and tackle people, or hold R and roll a snowball until it is big enough to flatten anyone in its way.

BLADE DASH

Tap to dash-slash. Every kill refills your dash, so the whole room can be one unbroken chain.

Every stage is one room drawn in ink on paper, full of enemies, and you clear it with a tap. Your samurai dashes wherever you point, the aim snaps onto anyone close to your line, and a kill refills both of your dash charges, so you can go from enemy to enemy without touching the ground. Landing breaks the chain. Swordsmen flash a red ! before they swing, archers draw a line at you before they loose, shield guards block from the front, barrel carriers blow up everyone near them, and generals take three hits.

Six floors of an unfinished tower over the harbour with downtown across the water. Fire it at the floor and it throws you; fire it at a man and it is a shotgun. Two shells, then a reload you have to find the time for.

NIGHT SHIFT

Do the job, and do not get caught. They have to kill you to stop you.

A top-down city at night, and a police force that does not look for YOU - it looks for a description, built one line at a time out of what you left at the scene: vehicle, clothing, face, prints, build. The screen before the job is the one that matters, because a ski mask denies them your face and gloves deny them your prints, and face or prints means identified, which means your name, your address and your bank card. Drive, get out, go in, take a different car.
