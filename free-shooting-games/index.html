IRONSIGHT

A realistic first-person shooter. Build your gun in the gunsmith, then fight nine bots in free-for-all or a 5v5 team match across four maps. Headshots kill instantly. Desktop only: needs a mouse and keyboard. Big download.

A tactical first-person shooter with realistic gloved hands, real-looking guns and four maps: a rainy city, an office tower, the docks and a desert compound. Every match has ten fighters: free-for-all against nine bots, or team deathmatch five against five. Bots fight each other too, hunt, and throw frag, flash and smoke grenades. A headshot is an instant kill. In the gunsmith you choose optics from iron sights to an 8x scope, muzzles, grips, laser colour and camo. The firing range is for trying builds. The online lobby needs the game running on your own computer, so it is switched off here. Desktop only, and the first load is large (around 100 MB per map).

EGG WARS

A claymation egg shooter. Crack the other eggs in a free-for-all against five bots, aim for the yolk for a YOLKSHOT, and level up to unlock new guns, shell colours and hats.

QUICKDRAW

Hand on the holster. One shot each, then hands down, until one of you drops.

Keep the pointer on your holster through the wait. Leaving early is a re-do rather than a death - but three of them in one fight and you stand through the next word with an empty hand while he takes his time over you. On DRAW you pull the pointer clear and the gun follows your HAND, not a crosshair on his chest: the shot goes out along the line from your hip through your hand and carries on, and your hand has to stay on your own side of the street, so aiming is an angle. One shot each, then hands go back down and you do it again, until somebody is on the floor - being slower than him is survivable, but only just, because a bullet in you starts a quarter of a second to answer it. Damage is per limb: a headshot is instant, an arm is that arm gone, a leg puts you on one knee. He is a person too, not a stopwatch - he fumbles the draw, jumps the word, and pulls shots wide, and the worse he is the more of all three.
