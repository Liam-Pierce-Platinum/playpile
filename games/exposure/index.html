Firing takes a photograph: the room lights for an instant and that instant stays on screen, cold and frozen, while everything real keeps moving in the dark. It doesn't fade — it comes apart a shade at a time until there's nothing left. A match is live but dim and costs you standing still. The glow is always on and reaches about an arm's length, enough to know you've walked into a wall. Every flash tells every crawler on the deck where you fired from. Clear the deck, find the hatch. Each deck is a checkpoint.
