YOUR SETUP

the deck — mouse, or the arrow keys

  • slide your weight to a tip, then flick sideways — it flips, and which way depends on which end you are over. Slow slides only move your foot.
  • push down in the middle — it spins flat. Over a tip instead, it goes end over end.
  • push down hard over a rail — stomp onto it and grind
  • push down on the ground, then back up — ollie
  • stop, or come back to the centre — it settles under your feet and you land. Past a sixth of a turn, letting go completes the rotation.

stuck? the TRICK SHEETS button plays every gesture back at you.

the skater

  • A / D push left and right, or brake
  • A / D in the air spin your body for 180s and 360s
  • hold down in a transition to pump for speed
  • hold down out over a tip to manual
  • SPACE pick the board up, or step back onto it
  • on foot — A/D or LEFT/RIGHT walk, UP hops, DOWN crouches. you can walk up anything.
  • your LEGS run out — about six pops back to back, then roll to get them back. buy better legs in the shop.
  • S in the air hold the board out belly-up, so you can actually see the graphic you paid for
  • 1 - 0 in the air hold a GRAB — indy, method, superman, christ air. the longer you hold it the more it pays, and it pays CASH.
  • ESC pause  ·  R bail out and reset
