LIGHTS OUT

320 × 240 · six switches the beam is not a light, it is a leash

LOOK

mouse turn (click the window first)
left / right turn without the mouse

ACT

F or click — torch on / off
WASD run — only with the torch off
Q or right click — throw a blanket
E flip a switch   R restart

Torch on and you cannot take a step — but anything your beam is actually on stops dead where it stands. Torch off and you can run, and everything in the house starts moving, and you cannot see any of it. Every switch on the far side of a room has to be paid for in seconds of darkness. The only other thing you can spend is a blanket: throw one over something and it spends four seconds getting out from under it, lights on or off. A room you have lit stays lit, and they will not walk into a lit room — so the house does not get easier because you got better at it, it gets smaller.
