HOW TO BOX

moving

  • A / D step left and right
  • W / S step up and back across the ring. punches only land when you are lined up - stepping off the line is a defence, cutting him off is an attack.

punching - the arrows

  • ← jab   ↑ cross   → lead hook   ↓ uppercut
  • a punch that connects can be cancelled straight into the next one. tap in a rhythm for combos: ← ↑ one-two, ← ↑ → one-two-three, ← ↑ → ↓ THE FULL FOUR, → ↓ hook-upper, ↑ → ↑ cross-hook-cross…
  • every punch in a chain hits harder. a missed punch has to come all the way home - and he can counter it.

defence

  • SHIFT hold the guard up. SHIFT + SPACE guard low for body shots - same key as going low yourself. uppercuts come up through the high guard.
  • Q SLIP, lean back: beats straights, uppercuts and overhands. hooks wrap round it.
  • E DUCK: beats straights and hooks. duck into an uppercut and it does 50% more.
  • dodge a punch and your next one is a COUNTER for 35% extra.

hold SPACE - go low

  • hold SPACE and every punch goes to the BODY: ← body jab, ↑ body cross, → liver hook, ↓ uppercut to the gut.
  • body shots drain his stamina and slow him down, and they go straight under a high guard. he has to drop his guard low to stop them - and then his head is open.
  • combos still chain while you change levels: SPACE+→ then → is the body-head hook.
  • it works the other way too: when he goes to your body, SHIFT + SPACE blocks low.

the fight

  • STARS fill as you land clean. F spends one on a STAR PUNCH.
  • STAMINA (yellow) pays for punches. out of gas: slow and weak. body shots drain it.
  • part of every hit is recoverable (the pale bar) - stay clean for a moment and it comes back.
  • knocked down? mash the arrows to beat the count. three knockdowns is a TKO.
  • every fighter has weaknesses: a punch he can't defend, a target that hurts, a habit that leaves him open. watch for OPEN and the flashing ! wind-up.
