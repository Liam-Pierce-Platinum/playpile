What PLAYPILE is

PLAYPILE is a small games site with one rule: every game on it was made for it. There are no embeds from other portals and no copies of games you have played elsewhere. Some are full projects built over weeks — a Formula 1 simulation with thirty-three circuits, a NASCAR game on twenty ovals, a first-person shooter up a thirty-one storey tower, an N64-style dungeon crawler, a boxing game with ten opponents and a horror game played by torchlight. The rest are quick games written to be finished in a sitting, and the classics rebuilt with the details that make them feel right.

Everything runs in the browser. There is nothing to install and no account to make, and your scores, unlocks and saved progress stay on your own machine. Each game has its own page with the controls, a written guide to how it plays, tips drawn from how the game actually works, and answers to the usual questions. The games are still being worked on — the updates page lists what has changed and when.

Every game on PLAYPILE

Action

  • KICKBACK — A sawed-off with two shells in it, and the shells are your legs.
  • NIGHT SHIFT — Do the job, and do not get caught. They have to kill you to stop you.

Adventure

  • DUNGEON QUEST — An N64 dungeon crawler: four levels, three classes, and a black dragon at the end.

Arcade

  • PENGUIN DASH — A penguin, a grey sea, and everything in it that eats penguins.
  • STACK — Drop each slab on the one below. What hangs over gets sliced off and falls.
  • DRIFTER — Rocks, a small ship, and bullets that cost the fuel you steer with.

Driving

  • APEX — Thirty-three real circuits, twenty-four cars, and air that decides the overtake.
  • NASCAR — Twenty ovals, forty cars, and a draft that decides it on the last lap.

Horror

  • EXPOSURE — The deck is pitch black. Your muzzle flash is the only light, and it freezes.

Puzzle

  • SWEEP — Minesweeper on a clock, at whatever size and difficulty you can stand.

Shooter

  • QUICKDRAW — Hand on the holster. One shot each, then hands down, until one of you drops.

Sport

  • SOUTHPAW — Arcade pixel boxing: ten opponents, a gym, and a wrestling bowl and a sumo ring besides.

  • SPIKE — Two jelly slimes, one ball, one net, first to eleven. Bring a friend.

Strategy

  • INKVALE — A tower defence painted in watercolour, where your towers paint the enemy and the colours mix.
