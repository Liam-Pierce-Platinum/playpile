First-person parkour over block courses floating in a painted sky. Hop across gaps, balance along beams, bounce on pads, slide on ice, cross lava stepping stones and ride moving lifts to the golden beacon at the end of each course. How to play

BLOCK WORLD is first-person parkour on block courses floating in a painted sky above a sea of clouds. Every block looks brushed with paint. Your goal on each course is to reach the golden beacon at the end.

Move with W A S D, jump with Space and look around with the mouse (click the game once to lock the pointer). Hold Shift to walk slowly. The right mouse button or F builds a clay block, and the left mouse button or Q takes a block you placed back. R respawns you at the last checkpoint, Esc or P pauses and M mutes.

On a phone, the left thumb stick moves, the right side of the screen looks around, and there are JUMP, BUILD and TAKE buttons.

Fall into the clouds or step on lava and you go back to the last gold checkpoint you touched.

Tips and strategy

Walk on the hard bits. Holding Shift slows you down, which makes thin beams and slippery ice much easier.

Recycle your blocks. Each course gives you a limited number of clay blocks. On a long build-a-bridge gap, place a block ahead, step onto it, then take back the one behind you and place it ahead again.

Blocks fix mistakes too. You can drop a block to catch a short jump or to make an awkward climb easier. Just make sure you have enough left for the gaps that need them.

Time the lifts. Moving lifts follow a set path. Wait on the edge until one comes to you rather than jumping at it.

Grab the gems on a second run. First learn the course; then go back for the gems and a faster time.

What's in it

Twelve courses that unlock in order, with hops, beams, bounce pads, ice, lava stepping stones, moving lifts, build-a-bridge gaps, gold checkpoints and gems. You earn stars for your time against each course's par, and your best times are saved.

After course 2 the Sandbox opens: pick blocks with 1-9, the mouse wheel or the hotbar, place them, and break anything with the left mouse button.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your stars, times and unlocks are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. Falling just sends you back to a checkpoint.

I ran out of blocks. Take some back with the left mouse button or Q; blocks you placed are returned to you.

Does it flash? There is a soft, warm flash over the screen when you fall and respawn.
