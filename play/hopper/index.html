HOPPER

One button, one long night on the rooftop , and a jump a high a you hold it.

An endless runner in pixel art, back on the rooftops at night: lit windows, neon, water towers and aerials going past in three depths. The jump is analogue - how long you hold is how high you go - and there is a dive for landing early. Coins arc over the gaps, and they buy what your runner wears.

Controls

  • Space / Click / ↑ / Wjump (hold for higher)
  • ↓ / S / Shiftdive
  • Ppause
Dive to land early. A short hop over a low gap beats a long one every time.

How to play

HOPPER is an endless runner across the rooftops at night. Your runner never stops; the city scrolls towards you, and your only job is to get from one roof to the next. Press to jump. Keep holding and the jump carries on rising for a fraction of a second longer, so a tap is a hop and a held press is a leap. Press down while in the air to dive, which pulls you back to the roof much faster.

Your score is distance, in metres. The run ends if you fall into a gap or run into anything on a roof. Roofs step up and down as you go, and the gaps vary, but the game never lays a gap wider than you can clear at your current speed, and a long gap is always followed by a wider roof to land on.

Coins hang in arcs over many of the wider gaps. They do not add to your score; they go into a running total that unlocks new jackets for your runner on the home screen.

Tips and strategy

  • Most gaps want a hop. A short jump gets you down sooner, and the sooner you are back on a roof, the sooner you can jump again.
  • Dive to fix a long jump. A dive pulls you down more than twice as hard as a normal fall. If a leap is going to carry you over the next roof or into something on it, dive and land early.
  • Do not jump at pigeons. A pigeon flies towards you at head height above the roof. Running along the roof, you pass underneath it safely; jumping is what puts you into it.
  • Everything else must be jumped. Signs, crates and pipes sit on the roof itself, and touching one ends the run.
  • You will always have room. Obstacles are only ever placed in the middle part of a roof, never on the edge where you land, so you can always see them before you have to jump.
  • Expect it to get quicker. Speed rises with distance and keeps rising until roughly 1,300 metres, where it settles at a little over twice the starting pace.
  • Coins follow the arc of the jump. Clearing a gap at the usual height tends to collect them without trying.

What's in it

One endless run, plus a wardrobe on the home screen. You can pick from six cap colours and four trouser colours. Three jackets are free from the start – red, blue and green – and three more unlock on coins collected across all your runs:

  • White: 40 coins
  • Gold: 150 coins
  • Neon: 400 coins

Locked jackets show a padlock; point at one to see its price. Your choice is kept between visits. Rooftop hazards come in four kinds: neon signs, crates, pipes and pigeons.

Questions

Does it save my distance and coins?

Yes, on this device. The home screen keeps a top-ten table of distances with three initials each. Your lifetime coin total and your outfit are remembered too.

Can I play it on a phone?

Yes for jumping: tap to hop, and keep your finger down for a higher jump. The dive needs a keyboard, though – the down arrow, S or Shift.

Which keys jump?

Space, Enter or a click starts a jump. Holding Space, the mouse button, the up arrow or W keeps it rising.

Can I pause?

P pauses, and the game pauses itself if you switch away. R abandons the run and returns to the home screen.
