How to play

KART CLASH is a kart battle in glossy toy plastic. You and five bots race around a studded baseplate arena surrounded by giant toys, and the goal is not to finish a lap but to knock out as many karts as you can. The game drops you straight into a match.

Drive with W A S D or the arrow keys. Drive through a ? box to pick up an item, then press Space (or J) to use it. Esc opens the garage menu. On a phone, the left thumb stick drives and the FIRE button uses your item.

A match lasts two minutes. Every kart you knock out is a KO, and the kart with the most KOs when time runs out wins. Knocked-out karts come back, including yours, so it is about scoring, not surviving.

Tips and strategy

Always be carrying something. You can only score with items, so plan a route that takes you through the next ? box as soon as you have used one.

Know your four items.

  • ROCKET: homes in on a target. Fire it when a kart is roughly ahead of you and let it do the steering.
  • GUMBALL GUN: 12 shots. Line up behind a kart and keep firing.
  • POP MINE: you get two and they drop behind you. Use them when you are being chased, or drop them on the path to a ? box.
  • BOING WAVE: a shockwave that only reaches karts close to you. Hold it until someone is right next to you.

Do not drive in straight lines when chased. Weaving makes the gumballs miss, and the arena's corners and toys break line of sight.

What's in it

Two-minute battle matches against five bots, four item types from the ? boxes, and a results screen. KOs and where you finish earn XP, and levelling up unlocks paint jobs for your kart, including Cherry, Tangerine, Lime, Sky, Grape, Bubblegum, Midnight and Gold Rush, which you pick in the garage. The bots get sharper as your level rises.

Everything is made to look like shiny plastic toys, with clear-coat reflections, a studded baseplate floor and oversized toys around the arena.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your level and paint jobs are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. They are toy karts, and a knocked-out kart simply comes back.

How do I change my paint job? Press Esc for the garage menu.

Can I play on a phone? Yes: a thumb stick on the left to drive and a FIRE button on the right.
