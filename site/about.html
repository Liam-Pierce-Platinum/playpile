Hey, welcome to PlayPile

A home for games you can open in a browser and get straight into

A pile of ideas that became games

PlayPile is Liam’s collection of browser games. Some are quick little score chasers. Others have whole worlds to explore. The fun is finding something you didn’t know you wanted to play.

Start with a category, follow a character or just look through the whole pile. You don’t need an account to play.

Your progress stays with you

Scores and profiles are saved in this browser. If you move to another device, use a save code from your profile menu. Clearing the browser’s saved data can clear your progress too.

Stuck on something?

Each game has controls and a guide underneath it. There’s a report link there too. If a game gets stuck or an instruction makes no sense, let us know which game and what happened.

Still growing

The updates page keeps track of additions and fixes. The dev log looks at how parts of the games work.

Say hello

Ideas, bug reports and the occasional very good score are welcome at support@playpile.net
