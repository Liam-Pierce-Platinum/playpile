About

Sixteen browser games in one place. Nothing to install, no account, and every game keeps your best score in your own browser.

The five big ones

HIGHRISE, KICKBACK, LIGHTS OUT, EXPOSURE and DUNGEON QUEST are full projects, each built over weeks. HIGHRISE is a thirty-one storey tower you climb floor by floor; DUNGEON QUEST is an N64-style crawler with four levels, three classes and a black dragon at the end of it.

The ten quick ones

Written to be finished in one sitting: a stacker, a breakout, an asteroids, a runner, a merge puzzle, a pipe puzzle, a minesweeper, a reaction duel, a hoops game and a volleyball. They all run on the same small engine, so they behave the same way on a phone as on a desktop, and all of them work with one finger.

Adverts

There are advertising slots on this site, and there are rules about them: none over a game that is being played, none that move, none that make a noise, and none inside the game itself. The only one you will meet mid-flow is a short panel while a game loads, and it is skippable.

Every game runs in your browser. Nothing installs, nothing needs an account, and your best scores are kept on your own machine.
