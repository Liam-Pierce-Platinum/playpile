Accessibility

What the site does to be usable by as many people as possible, and where it still falls short.

The site

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, and starts with a "Skip to the content" link.
  • Pages are written as real text, with headings and descriptions for every game, so screen readers can read them.
  • Text sizes follow your browser's zoom, and pages fit phone screens without scrolling sideways.
  • Adverts never play sound and never cover a game while it is being played.

The games

  • Every game can be paused, usually with P or Esc.
  • The controls for each game are listed on its page, under the game, before you start.
  • Most of the small games work with a single button, a mouse or a touch screen.
  • Games are visual and most need sight to play. The bigger 3D games need a reasonably recent computer; on slower machines they lower their own detail.

Flashing lights

A warning for people with photosensitive epilepsy. Some games contain bright flashes and rapidly changing light, which can trigger seizures in a small number of people. In particular: EXPOSURE (the screen lights up white with every shot), HIGHRISE, KICKBACK and QUICKDRAW (muzzle flashes), and the explosions in INKVALE, APEX and NASCAR, and bright hit and explosion flashes in BOMB SQUAD, PARRY and TINY HARBOR. If you or anyone in your family has epilepsy or has had a seizure, talk to a doctor before playing. Stop at once if you feel dizzy, have blurred vision, twitching or any loss of awareness.

Something does not work for you

Email us with the page or game and what got in the way. It will be fixed if it can be.
