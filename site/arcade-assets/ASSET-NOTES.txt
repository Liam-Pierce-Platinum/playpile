PLAYPILE category cast

The Arcade, Action, Adventure, Puzzle, Strategy, Horror, New, Popular and
Friends figures reuse the owner's Pavement Punchers full-body character art.
Their authored poses were cropped into small lossless WebP animation atlases.
The original project and its original files were left untouched.

Driving, Sport and Shooter are new six-pose sheets made with the built-in
image_gen tool, matching the arcade pixel-art direction. Exact prompts are
saved in generation-prompts.json. The sheets were packed at their actual UI
display size with nearest-neighbor sampling and retained transparency.

arcade-art.js owns playback, pauses offscreen characters, and presents a still
frame for reduced-motion visitors. No remote image URLs or runtime libraries
are used. Drawing helpers add small pixel props, including the arcade cabinet,
map table, lantern, puzzle blocks, coins and sparks.

Production assets: the thirteen .webp files in this folder.
cast-preview.png is an art contact sheet rather than a production dependency.
The two Python scripts document packing. They accept source directory paths
as arguments and require Pillow; they are not used by the deployed website.
