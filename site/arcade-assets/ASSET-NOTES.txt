PLAYPILE original category cast

All twelve categories now use original designs generated for PlayPile with
the built-in image_gen tool. Pavement Punchers informed the full-body arcade
pixel-art style only. None of its roster artwork is used in this cast.

Nine new six-pose atlases replace the reused roster: Arcade, Action,
Adventure, Puzzle, Strategy, Horror, New, Popular and Friends. Friends is
one synchronized two-person fist-bump animation. Exact prompts are in
original-cast-prompts.json. The new atlases are named *-original.png.
tools/pack-original-cast.ps1 mechanically packs the six generated cells into
horizontal strips with nearest-neighbor sampling and preserved alpha.

Driving, Sport and Shooter were already original designs generated for
PlayPile. Their six-frame .webp atlases remain, with their prompts in
generation-prompts.json. No character images are read from another project.

arcade-art.js owns playback, pauses offscreen characters, and presents a still
frame for reduced-motion visitors. All artwork is served locally. Small
canvas props include the arcade cabinet, map table, lantern and puzzle blocks.
