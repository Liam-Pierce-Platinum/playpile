Credits and licences

The games on PLAYPILE are made here, but they stand on some excellent free software. Thank you to the people who wrote it.

Software

  • three.js (release 169), the 3D engine behind APEX, NASCAR, HIGHRISE, DUNGEON QUEST, KICKBACK and several of the small games. Copyright 2010-2024 three.js authors. MIT licence.
  • PeerJS, which connects browsers for the nine online games. Copyright Michelle Bu and Eric Zhang. MIT licence. Online games are introduced through the free public PeerJS server.

Fonts

  • IM FELL English SC by Igino Marini, used in INKVALE. SIL Open Font License.
  • Patrick Hand by Patrick Wagesreiter, used in INKVALE. SIL Open Font License.

The arcade characters

The pixel lettering and several category characters come from Pavement Punchers, another of Liam’s game projects. The driving, football and soldier characters were made for PlayPile’s arcade menu. Their animations and props are part of the site.

Everything else

The games themselves - their code, art, music, sound and writing - are made for PLAYPILE. Most of the art is drawn by the games' own code as they load. If you believe something here is yours and is not credited properly, tell us and it will be put right.
