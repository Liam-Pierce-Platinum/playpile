How to play

ARCHER DUEL is an archery duel drawn like an old woodcut print, with carved lines, ink outlines and a ringed sun. Two archers stand on opposite sides of the screen, and the first to bring the other down wins.

To shoot, press anywhere with the mouse or a finger, drag back, and release. The further you drag, the more power the shot has, and a dotted line shows the arc your arrow will fly. The button at the bottom left opens the menu and the one at the bottom right mutes.

Where you hit matters. A head shot does the most damage, then the torso, then legs and arms. Arrows stick where they land, and a limb that is struck goes limp. When an archer goes down, they collapse as a ragdoll in slow motion.

Tips and strategy

Find the range first. Your first arrow tells you a lot. Note where it landed and adjust the drag length and angle for the next one.

Aim high. A head shot does close to twice the damage of a torso hit, so it is worth aiming for once you have the range.

Do not wait too long. In the ladder, your rival shoots in real time. A good-enough arrow now beats a perfect one later.

Read the wind. From stage 3 there is wind, which pushes arrows along the way. Aim against it.

Pick perks for your style. After each ladder win you choose one of three perks, such as Hawk Eye, Quick Nock, Twin Shaft, Heavy Heads, Iron Cap, Thick Hide, Field Mend and Red Thirst. They stack up as you climb.

What's in it

1P LADDER: real-time duels against computer archers that work out the arc and then miss by an amount that depends on their skill, so they get more accurate as you go. Wind starts at stage 3, you pick a perk after each win, and your best stage is saved.

2P DUEL: two players take turns on the same screen, and the wind changes every turn.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your best ladder stage is saved in your browser.

Is it violent? It is a duel with bows. Arrows stick in the archers and defeated archers collapse as ragdolls with ink splats, all in a black-and-white print style with no realistic blood.

Can two people play? Yes, in 2P DUEL, taking turns on one device.

Does it flash? No. There is a little screen shake on hits.
