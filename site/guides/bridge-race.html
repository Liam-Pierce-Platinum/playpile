How to play

BRIDGE RACE is a race to the top of a floating pastel island. You play the pink figure. On each floor there are bricks scattered around in four colours, and your job is to pick up the pink ones. You carry them in a stack on your back.

When you walk up a bridge, each step you take lays one of your bricks down as a stair. Run out of bricks halfway and you cannot go higher, so you have to go back down and collect more. The first figure to reach the golden top floor wins. There are three rivals, each building their own bridges.

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere on the screen to use it as a joystick. Esc or the II button opens the menu and the hat shop, and M mutes. On the results panel, Enter or Space takes you to the next level.

Tips and strategy

Collect before you climb. Look at the bridge and the size of your stack before you start up it. Climbing with too few bricks wastes time going back down.

Bump with care. If you run into a rival who is carrying fewer bricks than you, their stack falls off and scatters as white bricks, which anyone can pick up. If they are carrying more, it does not work in your favour, so check stack sizes first.

White bricks are free. After a bump, scoop up the white bricks before the rivals do. They count towards your bridge just like pink ones.

Clear a route. Plan a loop through the pink bricks instead of zig-zagging after each one, and pick the bridge closest to where your stack fills up.

What's in it

A level-by-level race where later levels add more floors to climb and faster rivals. Each race pays out coins, and the hat shop sells five hats to wear: a Party cone, a Bow, Bunny ears, a Crown and a Halo. Bricks fly into your stack, steps pop into place, and the winner gets confetti.

The look is soft matte clay with rounded shapes, a gradient sky and a gentle haze.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your level, coins and hats are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. Bumping just knocks bricks loose.

Which one am I? The pink figure, and you collect the pink bricks.

Does it flash? No. There is confetti and a little camera shake.
