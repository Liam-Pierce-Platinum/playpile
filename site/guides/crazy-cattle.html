How to play

CRAZY CATTLE is a battle royale for knitted sheep. Everything is made of felt and wool: the sheep have button eyes, and the arena is a patchwork quilt floating in the sky, held together with running stitches. Each round there are between 6 and 15 sheep on the quilt, and only one can win.

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys. Press Space (or Shift or J) to RAM: a quick dash that knocks back any sheep you hit. On a phone, use the left thumb stick and the RAM button. After a round, Enter goes to the next one.

Fall off the quilt and you are out. While you fight, the quilt unravels patch by patch from the outside in, so the space keeps getting smaller. The last sheep standing clears the field.

Tips and strategy

Hold the middle. The edge patches go first. Sheep near the edge are in danger from both the unravelling and from rams, so stay central and let the others fight out there.

Flustered sheep fly further. The knockback from a ram grows each time a sheep is hit again. A sheep that has just been rammed a couple of times is easy to finish off.

Do not ram towards the edge. If you miss, your own dash can carry you off. Ram from the inside out, so a miss leaves you safe.

Watch for two at once. When two sheep line up behind you, move away. Getting bounced between them is the fastest way off the quilt.

What's in it

Rounds of 6 to 15 sheep on a shrinking quilt. Each sheep you ram off earns +10 yarn, and yarn buys new wool coats: Cream, Coal, Rose, Mint, Sky, Mustard, Plum and Rainbow. Later levels bring bigger flocks, more aggressive sheep and a different-sized quilt. There are baas, bonks and ripping-wool sounds, and tufts of wool fly when sheep collide.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your level, yarn and coats are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. Knocked-off sheep fall away in a puff of wool.

Is there online multiplayer? Not yet. The other sheep are computer players.

Does it flash? No.
