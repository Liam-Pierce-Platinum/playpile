How to play

CUBE MERGE mixes the number puzzle 2048 with a snake game. You are a snake made of numbered cubes that look like cut gems, sliding around a crystal arena under a starry sky. The number on your head cube is what counts.

Your snake steers towards the mouse pointer, or towards your finger if you drag on a phone. You can also turn with A and D or the arrow keys. Hold the mouse button or Space (or the BOOST button on a phone) to dash.

Eat loose cubes that are worth the same as or less than your head. When two cubes of the same value meet, they fuse into one of double the value, just like in 2048: two 8s make a 16, two 16s make a 32, and so on. Reach the level target, starting at 128, to move up a level.

Tips and strategy

Keep your chain in order. Merges chain up best when your cubes run from biggest at the head to smallest at the tail. Eat the cube that matches your smallest one to set off a cascade.

Size up every head. You can bite the tail off a smaller snake and ram a smaller head to shatter it, and its cubes become food. But a bigger head can do the same to you, so steer clear of them.

Dash to escape or to steal. A dash gets you out of a bigger snake's path or onto a cube before a rival takes it.

Expect a tougher arena. Each level raises the target to the next power of two (128, 256, 512...) and the rival snakes get bigger and more aggressive.

What's in it

A level-by-level climb with targets that double each time, rival cube snakes that grow with you, and a result card when you are shattered, from which you can go straight back in. Your best score and the highest level you have unlocked are saved.

The cubes are shaded like gems, with chamfered edges, rainbow refraction and sparkling facets, on a crystal floor under a star dome. Broken snakes burst into star shards.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your best score and level are saved in your browser.

Do I need to know 2048? No. Just remember that two cubes with the same number join into one with double the number.

Is it violent? No. It is numbers and crystals.

Does it flash? No. There are sparkles, but no screen flashes.
