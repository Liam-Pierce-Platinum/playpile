How to play

DRIFT TOWER is a one-button driving game. A little clay car drives along a zigzag road that floats on pastel towers above the clouds. It is always moving, and it is always drifting either left or right.

Hold the mouse button, a finger on the screen or Space (or Left, Up or A) to drift left. Let go to drift right. That is the whole control scheme. Switch at each corner so the car follows the road. If it runs off the edge, it falls into the clouds and the run is over; tap or press DRIVE AGAIN to try again.

Your score is the number of corners you clear.

Tips and strategy

Switch at the corner. The car takes a moment to swing round, so change direction just as it reaches the bend. Too early and you cut off the edge; too late and you run off the end.

Find the rhythm. As your score rises the car speeds up and the straights get shorter. At that point it is easier to feel the beat of left-right-left than to react to each corner.

Look ahead. Keep your eyes on the next bend or two, not on the car.

Gems are a bonus. Gems on the road are saved even when you fall, so they always count. But a gem on the very edge is not worth losing a long run for.

Do not panic at a new zone. Every 20 points the colours change. The road works the same way, so keep your rhythm.

What's in it

An endless zigzag road with speed that rises with your score, and a new colour palette every 20 points, from dawn to dusk tones. Gems collected on the road are banked between runs and buy car colours in the GARAGE, including Butter, Peach, Mint, Sky, Plum and Cocoa.

Everything is soft matte clay with rounded shapes, puffs of clay tyre smoke, gem bursts and towers that fade into a pastel sky. The engine, skids and chimes are all made in the browser.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your best score, gems and car colours are saved in your browser.

Is there a brake? No. The only control is left or right.

Can I play on a phone? Yes. Hold your finger down to go left and lift it to go right.

Does it flash? No. There is a small shake when you fall.
