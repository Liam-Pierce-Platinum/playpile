How to play

EGG WARS is a first-person shooter where everyone is an egg, made to look like stop-motion clay, with fingerprints and lumps in the surface and a slightly jerky, hand-animated look. The game drops you straight into a match. It is a free-for-all: you against five bots, and every egg is against every other egg.

Move with W A S D and aim with the mouse; click once on the game to lock the pointer. Click to shoot, Space jumps and R reloads. Swap weapons with 1-3, Q or the mouse wheel, and hold the right mouse button to scope in with the Whisk. Tab shows the scoreboard and M opens the menu. On a phone, the left thumb stick moves, dragging on the right side looks around, and there are FIRE, JUMP, R, SWAP and SCOPE buttons.

A match is won by the first egg to 15 cracks, or by whoever leads when the 2:30 clock runs out. Your shell visibly cracks as you take damage. After the match you see the results and the XP you earned, and the next match starts.

Tips and strategy

Go for the yolk. A headshot is a YOLKSHOT and does double damage. Aiming at the top of the egg finishes fights much faster.

Do not stand still. Five bots are all looking for someone to crack. Strafe and jump while you shoot, and break line of sight to reload.

Pick the right gun. The Yolker (a shotgun, from level 2) wins close fights round corners. The Whisk (a sniper, from level 4) rewards patience at long range with its scope. Swap to suit where the fight is.

Watch the scoreboard. Near the end, cracking the leader matters more than padding your own score.

What's in it

Free-for-all matches against five bots, streak call-outs, a kill feed, a slow-motion finish and a results screen with XP. Levels unlock new weapons (the Yolker at level 2, the Whisk at level 4), shell colours such as Speckled, Free Range, Robin, Plum and Golden, and hats including a Beanie, a Bowler and a Party Cone. The bots get better as your level rises, so the game stays a fair fight.

From the menu you can also host an online room. You get a 4-letter code, and friends who type it in under JOIN play in your match, with your game running the bots and the clock.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your level, unlocks, shell colour and hat are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? It is a cartoon shooter, but the targets are clay eggs. They crack and break apart; there is no blood.

Can I talk to other players online? No. There is no chat. Online rooms are private and only people with your 4-letter code can join.

How do I open the menu? Press M during a match.
