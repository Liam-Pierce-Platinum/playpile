How to play

EGGY ROAD is a gentle-looking but tricky physics driving game, painted in watercolour. You drive a small truck over rolling hills, and there is an egg sitting loose in the back. It bounces and rolls with every bump. If it falls out and touches the ground, it cracks and your run ends.

Press Right, D or W for gas, and Left, A or S to brake or reverse. In the air, pressing gas leans the truck back. R restarts, and Space or Enter retries after a crack. On a phone, hold the right half of the screen for gas and the left half to brake.

Your run is measured in metres. You get a milestone every 100 m, and every 350 m you cross into a new painted region.

Tips and strategy

Ease over the crests. The egg flies out when the truck is thrown upward, which mostly happens at the top of a hill. Lift off the gas just before the crest and let the truck roll over.

Land level. If you do leave the ground, use the gas to lean back so both wheels land together. A nose-first landing bounces the egg out.

Brake on the way down. Speed builds fast on downhills, and the next bump will launch you. A touch of brake keeps things calm.

Spend your drops. Golden drops buy upgrades in the Garage, and each one has five levels. The Basket wall keeps the egg in, Soft springs soak up bumps, Tyres grip better on climbs, and the Engine gets you up steep hills. Walls and springs help most at the start.

What's in it

An endless road through five painted regions that repeat as you go further: Meadow Lane, Ochre Fields, Plum Dusk, Frost Hollow and Rose Dunes. Golden drops to collect, 100 m milestones, four upgrades with five levels each, and your best distance saved.

Every surface is painted by the game as a watercolour wash, with pigment pooling at the edges, paper grain and hills that bleed into the paper.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your best distance, drops and upgrades are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. The only casualty is the egg.

Does it flash? There is one short bright flash and a moment of slow motion when the egg cracks.

Why did my egg crack on a flat road? It probably bounced out on the last bump and rolled. Watch it in the bed and slow down when it starts to move.
