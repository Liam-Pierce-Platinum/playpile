How to play

FREEZE RUN is red light, green light in a pastel 3D playground. At the far end stands giant Marigold. While she sings with her back turned, everyone runs towards her. When she turns round, everyone must freeze. Anyone still moving while she is looking pops into confetti.

Hold Space, W or Up (or hold the mouse button or a finger) to run, and let go to freeze. Steer with A and D or the arrows, or by dragging while you hold on a phone. On the result card, Enter or Space moves on or retries; R retries and M mutes.

Reach the finish before the timer runs out to be SAFE!. Get spotted and it is CAUGHT!; run out of time and it is TIME UP. Either way you can try the level again.

Tips and strategy

Listen. Marigold's song resolves to its low "home" note right before she turns. Once you hear the tune heading home, let go.

Release early, not late. Stopping a fraction too early costs a few centimetres. Stopping a fraction too late costs the level.

Mind the clock too. You also have a timer, and stars are given for how much time is left. Being too careful can run you out of time on long fields.

Do not trust a twitch. From level 5 she sometimes does a fake half-turn. Wait until she is clearly facing away again before you set off.

Steer round the bumpers. From level 3 there are mushroom bumpers on the field. Bumping one wastes time, so plan a line round them while you are frozen.

What's in it

Twelve tuned levels, then endless levels after that. Each level is longer or harder than the last: longer fields, more runners beside you, shorter songs and longer stares. Mushroom bumpers arrive at level 3 and fake-outs at level 5. You earn one to three stars per level for the time left, and your stars and unlocked levels are saved.

The look is flat pastel shapes with ink outlines, like a picture book.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your stars and levels are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. Caught runners pop into confetti and paint splats.

Can I play with the sound off? Yes, but the song is your best warning that she is about to turn, so it is harder.

Does it flash? No.
