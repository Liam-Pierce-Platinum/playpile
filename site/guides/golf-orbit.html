How to play

GOLF ORBIT is a one-button golf game. Instead of a hole, there is a long chain of tiny resort islands, each curving like a little planet, set in a bright blue sea. You hit the ball as far as you can along the chain.

Press Space or tap once to lock the angle as the arrow swings, then again to lock the power as the meter fills. If you stop the power meter on the dashed sweet spot, you get a PERFECT shot with 15% extra. Once you have bought the Pocket Rocket, tapping while the ball is in the air fires a boost. M mutes.

The ball bounces and rolls over everything it lands on. The run ends when it stops, stalls, or falls into the sea, and the distance is your score.

Tips and strategy

Learn the surfaces. The sea skips the ball along, like a stone on a pond. Sand slows it down. Grass is in between. The giant beach balls bounce it hard.

Go for skips. A lower, faster shot tends to skip across water, while a high lob drops in and stops. Experiment with the angle.

Chase the extras. Floaties give the ball a boost, and starfish are coins. Both are worth bouncing through.

Spend your starfish. Upgrades make every run longer: Driver for more power, Spring Core for livelier bounces, Aero Dimples for better flight, Starfish Magnet to pull in coins, and Pocket Rocket for a mid-air boost.

Save the rocket. Fire the Pocket Rocket when the ball is about to drop into the sea or stall, to carry it on to the next island.

What's in it

An endless chain of islands with a new zone name every 350 m, from Tee Island and Coral Key through Mango Atoll, Lagoon Nine and Coconut Cay out to Far Horizon. The sky warms towards sunset the further you go. There are five upgrades to buy with starfish, and your progress is saved.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your starfish and upgrades are saved in your browser.

Is there a hole to get the ball in? No. It is a long-drive game: the further, the better.

Can I play on a phone? Yes. The whole game is tapping.

Does it flash? No.
