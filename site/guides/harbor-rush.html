Your first delivery

Start a shift at the dispatch yard. Ease into the pickup circle and slow down until the parcel loads. Follow the marker to the ferry terminal and slow down again to unload. You can stop after the payment or open Jobs for another run.

Choosing a route

The route line is a suggestion. A turn through an open block can save time, but hitting traffic can damage your parcel. Try a route in free roam before taking fragile cargo through it.
