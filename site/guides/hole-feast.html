How to play

HOLE FEAST is a timed arena game in a miniature model-town look, with the blurred edges of a tilt-shift photo. You are a hole in the ground. Steer it around the streets and anything smaller than the hole falls in and is gone. Each thing you swallow adds points and makes the hole bigger, so you can swallow bigger things.

The hole follows the mouse cursor: the further the cursor is from the hole, the faster it moves. You can also steer with W A S D or the arrow keys, or on a phone by dragging anywhere on the screen. M mutes. When a round ends, Enter or Space plays again.

You share the town with five rival holes. When the clock reaches zero, the hole with the most points wins.

Tips and strategy

Start small. At first you can only fit people, cones and little props. Sweep through crowded streets to grow quickly, and you will soon fit cars, then buses, then houses.

Keep the combo going. Swallowing things in quick succession builds a combo. Head for clusters instead of chasing one thing at a time.

Holes eat holes. A hole that is 20% bigger than another can swallow it for a bonus; the victim comes back somewhere else, smaller. Rivals will hunt you if they are bigger and run away if they are smaller, so watch the sizes around you.

Save the big stuff. Towers and skyscrapers are worth a lot. Grow enough to take them before the round ends rather than circling the same streets.

What's in it

Three cities, each unlocked by finishing first in the one before: Pebble Town, Brickton and Glass Bay. There are combos, size-ups as you grow, and five rivals that forage, chase and flee. Six hole skins unlock as your lifetime feast points add up.

The town is painted like a toy model, with a soft blur at the top and bottom of the screen, rich colours and a marimba soundtrack.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your cities, skins and points are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. Things, including tiny people, just drop into the hole and disappear. There is no blood.

How do I unlock the next city? Finish first in the current one.

Does it flash? No.
