How to play

IRONSIGHT is a realistic first-person shooter. Press PLAY, pick a map, a mode and a difficulty, and you deploy straight into a match. Click to capture the mouse, then move with WASD, aim with the mouse, shoot with the left button and aim down the sights with the right. A headshot kills instantly, so careful aim beats spraying.

Modes and maps

Free for all puts you against nine bots: the first to the points target, or the leader when the clock runs out, wins. Team deathmatch is five against five. Kills, headshots and assists give points, and you respawn a few seconds after dying at the safest spawn. The maps are a rainy city at night, an office tower, the docks and a desert compound, each with three lanes.

Gunsmith

The gunsmith lets you build your loadout: a primary and a secondary gun, a lethal grenade (frag or semtex) and a tactical one (flash or smoke). Slide optics along the rail, from iron sights and red dots to an 8x scope, and choose muzzles, grips, laser colour and camo. Builds save automatically. The firing range is a quiet place to try them.

Tips

Lean with Q and E to peek round corners without stepping out. Slide (C while sprinting) to get into cover fast. Throw a flash before pushing into a room and smoke to cross open streets. Bots fight each other too, so let them thin each other out.

Questions

Why is Online greyed out? Online lobbies need the game's own server, which only runs when the game is installed on a computer. Matches against bots work fully here.

Does it work on a phone? No. It needs a mouse and keyboard, and the download is large.
