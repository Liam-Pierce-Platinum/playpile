How to play

You start in the long hallway in the middle of a dark house. Six rooms open off it, and the house has six light switches spread between them. Find every switch and turn it on with E, and you get out. If one of the things in the house reaches you first, the run is over and the screen tells you how many lights you managed.

Everything turns on the torch. With it on, you cannot take a single step. You can turn, and anything in the beam freezes for as long as you hold it there. With the torch off you can run, but so can they, and you cannot see where they are. Every switch has to be paid for in seconds of darkness.

A room whose switch you have flipped stays lit for the rest of the run, and the things will not walk into a lit room from outside it. The house does not get kinder. It gets smaller.

There is one mode: a single house, one run at a time. Click GO IN on the home screen to start, and press R after a death or an escape to go again.

Tips and strategy

  • The beam is narrower than it looks. Only something roughly in the middle of your view, with no wall between you and it, is pinned. The reticle in the centre of the screen is your aim.
  • Turning the torch on still helps when nothing is in the beam. Anything you are not pointing at keeps coming, but at a little over half the speed it moves in the dark.
  • They move in lurches: a burst of fast steps lasting around a second, then a stall of under a second. Cross open floor during the stall and check again before the next burst.
  • They head straight for you rather than finding a way round. A wall between you and one of them holds it up, so put walls in its way.
  • A blanket is the only thing that works in the dark. Press Q and it goes over the nearest thing roughly in front of you, if it is fairly close. A covered thing is out for four seconds and cannot hurt you, even if you walk right past it. If nothing is in front of you, the blanket is wasted.
  • You start with two blankets. Two more lie in the hallway, one towards each end; walk over them to pick them up.
  • Listen. A breathing sound starts when something is getting near and speeds up as it closes in. A screech means one is very close and your torch is off. Turn round and switch on.
  • Both things start in the two rooms along the bottom of the house. The three rooms across the top have nothing in them at the start, so they are the cheapest switches to take first.

What's in it

  • One house: a central hallway with three rooms along the top, a small room at the bottom left and a large room at the bottom right. The hallway has no switch, so it is never lit.
  • Six switches. The bottom-right room has two of them. The other four rooms each have one.
  • Two creatures, each nine feet tall, faceless and nearly black.
  • Four blankets in all: the two you carry in and the two in the hallway.
  • A top-ten leaderboard on the home screen.

Questions

What does the leaderboard measure?

Seconds spent running in the dark on a run where you got out. Lower is better. Time standing still does not count, whether the torch is on or off, so careful play is never punished.

Does it save my scores?

Yes. The top ten escapes and your initials are kept in this browser on this device. The board is not shared online, and clearing your site data wipes it.

Can I play it on a phone or with a controller?

No. It needs a keyboard and mouse. The left and right arrow keys can turn instead of the mouse, and Space also works the torch.

Why can't I walk?

Your torch is on. The bottom of the screen says TORCH ON - YOU CANNOT MOVE. Press F or click to turn it off, and W A S D will work again.

I pressed E and nothing happened.

You need to be standing right up against the switch. When you are close enough, E - TURN IT ON appears on screen.
