How to run a shift at Luke’s Diner

Start at the title screen, choose a stop on the Stars Hollow map, and clock in. The first shift teaches you the basics. Click or tap a customer to see what they need, then use the coffee pot or the kitchen window as the order calls for it.

As more seats fill up, watch the order bubbles and keep an eye on anyone who has been waiting. If you want time to learn where everything is, switch on Relaxed mode before clocking in.

Luke’s Diner is an unofficial Gilmore Girls fan game. It is unaffiliated with Warner Bros. and the makers of Gilmore Girls.
