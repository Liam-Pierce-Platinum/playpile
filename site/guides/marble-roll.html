How to play

MARBLE ROLL is a balance game with a glass cat's-eye marble. You roll it along courses of polished stone with brass trim that float high above a sea of clouds, from the start to a swirling portal at the finish.

Hold the mouse button or a finger on the screen to roll forward, and drag sideways to steer. On a keyboard, W or Up rolls, A D or Left Right steer, and S or Down brakes. R restarts the course, Esc opens the menu and M mutes.

Roll off the edge and you drop back to the last checkpoint arch you passed. Reach the portal to clear the course.

Tips and strategy

Momentum is everything. A glass marble keeps rolling. Let go or brake before narrow beams and tight turns, and steer with small movements.

Line up before you commit. On a beam, get the marble straight before you speed up. Correcting halfway along is how most falls happen.

Speed for the gaps. Jump gaps need a run-up. Build speed on the straight before a gap and keep it straight as you leave the edge.

Time the moving parts. Sweepers swing across the course and pushers shove from the side. Wait for one to pass, then roll through behind it.

Fewer falls, more stars. Stars on each course depend on how many times you fall, so a slow clean run is worth more than a fast messy one.

What's in it

Fifteen courses in four sky themes, Dawn, Noon, Sunset and Dusk, with narrow beams, diagonal shifts, zigzags, drops, jump gaps, sweepers, pushers and pillar walls. There are checkpoint arches, gems to collect and stars for each course, and new courses unlock as you clear them. Gems buy six glass marbles, including Clear Eye, Ruby, Cobalt, Jade, Amber and Opal Night. Your progress is saved.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your courses, stars, gems and marbles are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. It is a marble.

Can I play on a phone? Yes. Hold a finger down to roll and drag to steer.

Does it flash? No.
