How to play

MOTO STUNT is a side-view stunt motorbike game drawn like a comic book, with halftone dots, ink outlines and bright sunburst skies. Ride from the start to the finish of each level as fast as you can.

Up or W is the gas, and Down or S brakes and reverses. Left or A leans back, and in the air that turns into a backflip. Right or D leans forward for a frontflip. R restarts the level and P or Esc pauses. On a phone there are on-screen lean buttons plus GO and brake.

If the rider's head hits the ground, or you hit a barrel, a swinging hammer or a spike pit, you crash and go back to the last checkpoint flag.

Tips and strategy

Flips are time. Every full flip you land takes one second off your time. Three stars on a level usually needs flips, not just clean riding.

Land on your wheels. A flip only counts if you survive it. Start leaning early on a big jump so you have time to come back round, then let go of the lean and level the bike before you touch down.

Use the lean on the ground too. Leaning back lifts the front wheel and leaning forward keeps it down on steep slopes. Small corrections stop you looping over on hills.

Time the hammers. Swinging hammers move on a rhythm. Slow down, watch one swing, then go through behind it.

Do not panic at a crash. You restart at the last flag you passed, not at the start, so a crash late in a level only costs a little.

What's in it

Twelve levels with jumps, hills, barrels, swinging hammers and spike pits, in comic settings such as Mesa Noon, Sunset Junk and Night Pop. There are checkpoint flags, flip counting, stars for your time and unlocks as you progress, all saved on your device.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your stars and unlocks are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. Crashes are comic-book "booms" and the rider just respawns at the flag.

Does it flash? There is a bright comic "boom" flash and screen shake on each crash.

Why did my flip not count? You have to land it. If you crash on landing, the flip is lost.
