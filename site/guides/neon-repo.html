Getting a car home

Pick a call from dispatch and drive the scout car to the pickup marker. Slow down until the recovery prompt appears, then press E or tap the prompt to switch cars. Bring the recovered vehicle to its marked destination and use the same control to hand it over.

Protecting a payout

Leave braking room if you collect a van. In a faster car, wait for a straight before boosting. Condition and clean driving affect the payout. Between calls, the garage lets you put that money into your next shift.
