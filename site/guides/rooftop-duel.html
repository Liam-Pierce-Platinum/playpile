How to play

ROOFTOP DUEL is a two-button duel between two chunky, wobbly voxel fighters on a rooftop. The aim is not to shoot your rival down but to knock them off the roof. Every hit pushes them back, and the one who goes over the edge loses the round.

With one player, W, Space or Up jumps and D, J, F or Enter fires. With two players on one keyboard, player 1 uses W to jump and D to fire, and player 2 uses Up to jump and Left to fire. On a phone there are JUMP and FIRE buttons (four of them in two-player mode). Esc or P opens the menu.

The catch: you jump the way you are leaning, and your gun sways on its own. You do not aim; you choose when to pull the trigger. Land badly from a jump and you fall over, and you have to press jump to get back up.

Tips and strategy

Time the sway. Watch the gun swing and fire as it passes over your rival. One well-timed shot beats three wild ones.

Jump with a purpose. Jumping dodges shots and gets you back from the edge, but a bad landing knocks you over. Jump when you need to, not all the time.

Know the weapon. It changes every round: the revolver is steady, the shotgun is strong up close, the sniper is a big single push, the burp gun fires automatically, and the boom tube lobs an explosive in an arc.

Watch the clock. After 32 seconds a round turns into HIGH WINDS, which push both fighters outward. Get away from the edge before then.

What's in it

An arcade ladder of eight rivals, first to 3 rounds each: ROOKIE RAY, CHIMNEY SUE, SKIP GUTTER, MONA LEDGE, BIG TONY, DR. VENT, THE ANTENNA and BOSS PENTHOUSE. Each rival you beat unlocks their hat. There is also a Quick Duel and a two-player mode, both first to 5.

Five rooftops: TAR ROOF SUNSET, THE GAP, NEON HEIGHTS with a raised block, slippery FROST LEDGE and STORM DECK in the rain.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your ladder progress and hats are saved in your browser.

Can two people play? Yes, on one keyboard or with four buttons on one phone.

Is it violent? It is a cartoon gun duel with blocky toy-like fighters. Hits push fighters back; there is no blood.

Does it flash? A fighter flashes briefly when hit.
