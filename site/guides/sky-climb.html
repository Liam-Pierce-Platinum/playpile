How to play

SKY CLIMB is a calm third-person climbing game. A giant tower of floating junk rises out of a sea of clouds into a soft, hazy sky, and your little climber with a long scarf has to get to the top and ring the bell. There are no enemies and no lives. The only danger is falling.

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys, and jump with Space: hold it for a full-height jump or tap it for a short hop. Shift sprints. Look around with the mouse (click to lock the pointer) and zoom with the wheel. M mutes.

If you fall into the clouds, you go back to your pin. Press F to plant it; you are given one pin for each new zone you reach. R returns you to your pin, or to the start if you have none. On a phone, the left half of the screen is a stick, the right half looks around, and there are jump and pin buttons.

Tips and strategy

Pin early. As soon as you reach steady ground in a new zone, plant your pin. A fall then costs you a few platforms instead of the whole zone.

Control your jump height. Most of the climb is short hops. Save the full held jump and the sprint for real gaps, because overshooting is as bad as falling short.

Use the camera. Turn the view to see where the next platform really is before you jump. Zoom out to plan a route.

Collect sky shards. They stay with you between climbs. Six of them unlock Scarf Glide: hold Space while falling to glide, which rescues a lot of bad jumps.

What's in it

Six zones, each with its own junk and haze colour: The Scrap Shore, Pipe Gardens, Laundry Winds, Paper Kite Reach, The Drowned Library and the Bell of Stillness at the top. There are 36 sky shards to find. Six unlock Scarf Glide, and 16 and 28 re-weave your scarf in new colours. Your best height and best time are saved.

The look is soft and dreamy: haze that thickens with height, shafts of sunlight, muted film colours, a cloud sea below and a scarf that streams behind you.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your shards, best height and best time are saved in your browser.

Can I lose? No. Falling just costs height. You can keep climbing as long as you like.

Is it violent? No.

Does it flash? No.
