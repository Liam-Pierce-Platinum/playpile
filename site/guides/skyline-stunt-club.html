Landing the first jump

Start at the clubhouse, face the marked ramp and build speed on the approach. Steer in the air to rotate. Aim to come down on a roof. For a safer crossing, use the bridge between decks.

Keeping a run going

Look for the next ramp as you land to chain stunts. Start a timed run when you know the layout, then follow its beacons in order. The garage shows which paint jobs your reputation has unlocked.
