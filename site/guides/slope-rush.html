How to play

SLOPE RUSH is an endless downhill run. You steer a glowing ball down a twisting neon-grid track that hangs in the air in front of a striped synthwave sun and wireframe mountains. The ball rolls by itself and keeps getting faster. All you do is steer.

Steer with A and D or the Left and Right arrows. On a phone, hold the left or right half of the screen. P or Esc pauses, M mutes, and Space or a tap starts a new run after you crash.

The run ends when you roll off the edge or hit a red block. Your score is the distance in metres plus bonuses: +10 for every gold shard you collect and +5 for every close pass by a block.

Tips and strategy

Steer early and small. At high speed, a big late swerve throws the ball across the track and off the other side. Little taps keep it under control.

Look ahead, not at the ball. Holes, drop-offs and bridges come up fast. Keep your eyes a little way down the track so you are already lined up when they arrive.

Close passes are worth points. Brushing past a red block earns +5, but only if you do not touch it. Take the easy ones and leave the risky ones.

Shards are a bonus, not the goal. A shard is worth ten points and also counts towards new skins, but distance is what builds a big score. Only grab shards that are already on your line.

Expect a change every 400 m. Each new sector narrows the track and adds more holes and blocks, so the first few seconds after the colours change are the most dangerous.

What's in it

One endless slope, split into 400 m sectors. Each sector brings a new colour palette, with names like NEON DRIVE, MIAMI DUSK, ICE GRID, ACID LINE and ULTRAVIOLET, and adds more hazards: holes in the track, gaps you drop off, red blocks and narrow bridges. Gold shards you collect are saved between runs and unlock new ball skins on the title menu, such as HOTLINE, SOLAR, VOID, TOXIC and PRISM.

The look is hand-written neon: a glowing grid track with sweeping pulses of light, wireframe red blocks and mountains, bloom, scanlines and a synthwave soundtrack made in the browser.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your best score, shards and chosen skin are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. You are a ball.

Does it flash? Yes. The screen flashes when you crash and briefly when the colours change at each new sector, and there is glow, scanlines and screen shake.

How do I change my ball? Skins are picked on the title menu once you have collected enough shards.
