How to play

SNAKE ARENA is a snake brawl on a dark sea, with glowing, bioluminescent snakes. You share the water with 24 computer snakes, and the goal is to grow as long as you can.

Your snake steers towards the mouse cursor. You can also turn with A and D or the arrow keys. Hold the left mouse button, Space, W or Up to SURGE: you go faster, but you shed length as orbs while you do. On a phone, touch and drag to steer and use the SURGE button.

Eat the glowing orbs to grow. The one big rule: any head that touches another snake's body dies and bursts into orbs. That includes yours. You also die if you leave the arena's pink ring.

Tips and strategy

Your body is your weapon. You cannot bite other snakes. Instead, get your body in front of their head so they run into it. Surge ahead of a rival and curl across its path.

Feast after a crash. A snake that dies bursts into lots of orbs. Be nearby to sweep them up, but watch out, because every other snake wants them too.

Do not cut in front of big snakes. They have long bodies that can wrap around you. Give them room.

Surge sparingly. It costs length, so use it to escape or to set up a cut-off, not to cruise around.

Watch the minimap. It shows where the ring and the other snakes are, so you are not surprised at the edge.

What's in it

A live leaderboard, a minimap, milestones as you grow, and 24 computer snakes that hunt, try to cut you off, avoid danger and surge. At the end of a run you earn pearls: your length divided by five, plus five for each kill. Pearls raise your rank through Plankton, Krill, Shrimp, Eel, Anglerfish and Siphonophore to Leviathan, and unlock five more skins: Coral Ribbon, Toxin Worm, Ember Siphon, Void Violet and Prism Leviathan.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your best length, pearls and skins are saved in your browser.

Are the other snakes real people? No, they are all computer players.

Is it violent? No. A crashed snake bursts into glowing orbs.

Does it flash? There is a pink flash over the screen when you die.
