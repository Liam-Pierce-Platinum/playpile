How to play

STICK SWING is a one-button swinging game, made to look like folded paper and layered card scenery. Your little paper figure flies through the air, and you hook onto stars to swing from them.

Hold the mouse button, a finger on the screen, or Space to throw a line to the nearest star and swing on it. Let go to release and fly. Hook the next star, swing, let go, and keep going until you reach the finish. R restarts the level, Esc opens the menu and M mutes.

You can land and roll on platforms, but if you fall off the bottom of the screen you start the level again. Hitting a pillar or a hanging blade knocks you back and breaks your hook.

Tips and strategy

Release on the way up. Letting go just after the lowest point of the swing sends you forward and upward. Letting go too late throws you up and backwards; too early sends you low.

The nearest star is the one you get. You do not aim; the line goes to whichever star is closest (and in reach) when you press. Time your press for when the star you want is the nearest one ahead.

Use bounce pads. They throw you back up into the air, which can save a run after a bad release.

Watch the obstacles. Floor pillars and hanging blades knock you back and drop you off your hook, and moving hooks shift while you aim for them. A short, quick swing past them is often safer than a big looping one.

Go for time once you know the level. Stars are given for how fast you finish compared with a par time, so a replay with fewer, cleaner swings can earn the full set.

What's in it

Thirty levels across five paper worlds: Dawn Fold, Maple Ridge, Jade Hills, Snow Paper and Dusk Crane. Every level has been played through by a bot to make sure it can be finished. Obstacles include bounce pads, floor pillars, hanging blades and moving hooks. You earn stars for speed, and a new paper skin unlocks every five levels.

The look is paper all the way through: grain and fibres in the paper, folded facets that catch the light, card scenery with drop shadows, a streamer trail behind you and paper confetti at the finish.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your levels, stars and skins are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. Obstacles just knock you back, and a fall restarts the level.

Can I play on a phone? Yes. Hold your finger on the screen to hook and lift it to let go.

Does it flash? No.
