How to play

STUNT CITY is a free-roam stunt park in vaporwave colours. You drive a chrome car across a shiny grid floor under a striped sunset, and the park is full of ramps, loops and rings. There is no track: go anywhere, hit anything, and get as much air as you can.

Drive with W A S D or the arrow keys. Shift fires the nitro and Space is the handbrake for drifting. In the air, W and S flip the car forwards and backwards, A and D spin it, and Q and E barrel-roll it. Land with the wheels down or you wipe out. R puts you back at the last safe spot, G opens the garage and M mutes.

On a phone there are on-screen steering, GAS, BRK and NOS buttons. In the air, GAS and BRK flip the car and the arrows spin it.

Tips and strategy

Stop rotating before you land. Let go of the flip or spin keys a moment early so the car has time to settle flat. A clean landing banks the stunt; a wipe-out loses your combo.

Speed is air. Hit ramps with nitro for longer, higher jumps, which means time for more rotations and more cash.

Use the challenges. Press Enter, or drive onto a glowing pad, to warp to the next challenge. Challenges pay well and teach you what the car can do. Esc quits a challenge if you want to free-roam again.

Upgrade the engine first. More speed makes the loops and big-air challenges much easier. Then grow the nitro tank.

What's in it

A free-roam park and eight challenges in order: SUNSET SPRINT, CHROME LOOP, BIG AIR, BACKFLIP, GAP JUMP, GRAND LOOP, SKY GAUNTLET and STUNT SHOW, covering rings, loops, big air, a backflip, a gap jump and a stunt-score round. Stunts and challenges pay cash, and the garage sells engine and nitro-tank upgrades and five chrome paints.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your cash, upgrades, paints and challenges are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. There are no people, and a wipe-out just resets the car.

I am stuck upside down. Press R to go back to the last safe spot.

Does it flash? No. It is bright neon, but there are no flashing effects.
