How to play

SUBWAY RUN is an endless runner through a train yard at sunset, painted in a graffiti cel-shaded style with spray-paint tags and pieces on every wall and train. Your runner sprints forward on their own across three lanes, and you dodge whatever is in the way.

Switch lanes with Left and Right (or A and D). Jump with Up, W or Space, and roll with Down or S; rolling in the air makes you drop fast. M mutes. On a phone, swipe left, right, up or down, or tap to jump.

Run straight into a train or a concrete block and you are BUSTED. Clipping the side of a train makes you stumble and the screen flashes; a second stumble within 3.5 seconds ends the run.

Tips and strategy

Read the obstacle. Low hurdles need a jump, high bars need a roll, and concrete blocks need a lane change. Trains need a lane change, unless you jump onto them from a ramp.

Listen for the horn. Some trains drive towards you and sound their horn. They close twice as fast, so move early.

Take the high road. Ramps lead up onto the train roofs, which are three trains wide and full of coins, and there is less to dodge up there.

Play safe after a stumble. For 3.5 seconds after a stumble, any second one ends the run. Stay in a clear lane until it passes.

Do the crew tags. Each rank has three missions. Clearing all three ranks you up and raises your score multiplier for every run after.

What's in it

An endless yard of trains, moving trains, hurdles, high bars, concrete blocks and rooftop runs. Spray-can power-ups give MAGNET, SPRING KICKS (high jumps onto trains) and 2X SCORE. Coins bank between runs and buy longer power-ups, six levels each. Crew tags (missions) rank you up and raise your multiplier. There is a boom-bap beat made in the browser.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your coins, upgrades and rank are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. A crash just shows the BUSTED card.

Does it flash? Yes. The screen flashes red on a crash and orange on a stumble.

Can I play on a phone? Yes, with swipes.
