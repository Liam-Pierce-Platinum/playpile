How to play

STILLSHOT is a first-person shooter with one big rule: time moves only when you move. Stand still and everything freezes, including the bullets in the air. Walk, and time runs at full speed. Looking around lets time trickle forward slowly, and every shot you fire gives it a brief burst.

Move with W A S D, look with the mouse, and click to shoot. Q or 1-3 switches weapons once you have more than one. R retries and M mutes. On a phone, the left stick moves, dragging on the right side looks around, and there are FIRE and GUN buttons.

The enemies are red crystal figures in a stark white room. One shot shatters them. But one hit from them and you are out too, and the level starts again. Clear every enemy to finish the level.

Tips and strategy

Stop and think. Because time is frozen while you stand still, you can always pause, look around and plan. Find every enemy and every bullet before you take a step.

Move as little as possible. Every step lets the enemies act. A small sidestep out of a bullet's line is better than a long run.

Mind the burst. Each shot moves time forward a little. Firing at a target that is far away lets the room move on while your bullet travels.

Dodge sideways. Bullets travel in straight lines. Stepping across their path clears them much faster than backing away.

Watch the runners. From level 3, melee runners charge you. Shoot them early, before they close the gap.

What's in it

Endless levels that build up in waves, with melee runners from level 3. You start with the PISTOL, unlock the SCATTER gun at level 4 and the RAPID gun at level 7. Your best level is saved.

The look is minimal on purpose: a white room with a faint floor grid and soft shading, and faceted red crystal enemies that glint and shatter. The music's pitch follows the speed of time.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your progress is saved in your browser.

Is it violent? It is a shooter, but the enemies are abstract red crystal figures that shatter into shards. There is no blood.

Why is the game called STILLSHOT? Because the world stays still until you move, and you aim in the stillness.

Does it flash? There are muzzle flashes and a brief screen flash when you are hit.
