How to play

TUNNEL DIVE is a reflex game in pure black and white. You fly down a tunnel covered in dizzying op-art patterns, and gates with gaps in them come rushing towards you. You cannot move yourself; instead you rotate the whole tunnel so the gap lines up with you.

Rotate with A and D or the Left and Right arrows. On a phone, hold the left or right half of the screen. P or Esc pauses and M mutes. If you hit a barrier the run is over; press Space, Enter or tap to go again.

Health warning: this game is made of fast, rotating, high-contrast stripes and checks, and the screen flashes on a crash and at each new zone. If patterns like that make you feel unwell, please choose a different game.

Tips and strategy

Look one gate ahead. By the time a gate is close it is too late to turn far. Line up for the next gap while you are still passing the current one.

Turn the short way. The gap is always reachable both ways round, so take the shorter rotation and save time for the gate after.

Protect your multiplier. Your score is distance times a multiplier from x1 to x8. You gain +1 for every 10 clean gates, so long clean streaks are worth much more than any single bonus.

Bonuses are a gamble. Passing very close to a barrier is a CLOSE call worth +25, and grabbing a PRISM is worth +50. Take them when they are on your line; do not swing across for them.

Be ready at 700 m. Each new zone speeds up, narrows the gaps and brings in spinning gates, which move while you approach them.

What's in it

An endless dive split into zones every 700 metres. The tunnel's pattern changes from zone to zone between CHECKER, MOIRE, SPIRAL and FAN, and each zone is faster and tighter than the last. Your best score and the deepest zone you have reached are saved.

Questions

Do I need an account? No. Your best score and deepest zone are saved in your browser.

Is it violent? No. There are no characters at all.

Does it flash? Yes, and more importantly the whole game is fast, rotating, high-contrast black-and-white patterns. It is not suitable for anyone sensitive to flashing or patterns.

Is there an autopilot? No, every gate is yours to thread.
