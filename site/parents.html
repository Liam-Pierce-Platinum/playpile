For parents

What your child will find on PLAYPILE, game by game, and how the site works behind the scenes.

The short version

  • Every game is free and plays in the browser. Nothing to download, nothing to buy, no in-game purchases.
  • No account is needed for anything, and the site never asks for a name, age or email to play.
  • There is no chat and no way to message other players.
  • Two games, HOOPS and INKVALE, can be played online with a friend who has been given a four-letter room code. Nobody can join without that code.
  • The site shows adverts from Google. They are never placed over a game while it is being played.
  • PLAYPILE is made for a general audience, not specifically for children under 13. Some games contain violence, listed below.

Privacy and children

Scores and progress are kept in the browser on your own device. A child can play every game without an account. If an account was made by a child under 13, email us and it will be deleted. You can see and delete everything the site has saved in a browser on the privacy settings page, and choose non-personalised adverts there too.

Screen time and wellbeing

Every game can be paused, and none of them punish you for stopping. None use daily login rewards, timers that make you come back, or anything to buy. Some games have bright flashes; the accessibility page lists them.
