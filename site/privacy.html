Privacy

The short version: your scores and profiles are saved in your own browser. Use a save code to move progress to another device. Email-and-password accounts and automatic cloud saves are not currently available. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits and page performance without analytics cookies. We do not sell your saved game progress. Google advertising may use cookies and other identifiers, as explained below.

What is stored, and where

Games keep scores, names you enter, unlocks and settings in your browser's own local storage. This saved game data is not uploaded to PLAYPILE. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it. Scores do not follow you to another browser or device automatically; you can transfer them with a save code.

Playing as a guest, your scores and progress stay on your machine. Your browser also contacts Cloudflare for aggregate visit and performance measurements, Google for advertising, and a matchmaking service and the other player if you choose to play online. These connections are explained below; they do not upload your saved game progress.

Profiles, save codes and accounts

The player button at the top of the page opens your profile panel. The current site uses local profiles, not online accounts:

A profile lets several people share one browser. Each profile keeps its own copy of those same local-storage keys. It never leaves the device and needs no email address.

A save code is your progress written out as a long line of text you can copy. Paste it into the panel on another device and it loads there. We never see it — copying and pasting it is entirely between you and your own clipboard.

Online accounts and cloud saves are currently switched off. The site does not currently collect an email address or password to save progress, and it does not upload profiles or scores to Firebase. This policy will be updated before online accounts or public online leaderboards are made available.

The saved progress is exactly what the games already keep on your device: high scores, the three letters you type in beside them, coins, unlocked clothes and settings. No location, no contacts, no device fingerprinting, nothing about what else you do online.

Levels, badges, the daily challenge and leaderboards

The site itself also keeps a few things in that same local storage, under keys beginning pd.site.: how many minutes you have spent in each game and on which days (used for your XP, level and play streak), which badges you have earned, your daily challenge streak, the games you mark as favourites, the picture you choose, and the list of games you played recently. It is counted in your browser while a game page is open in front of you, it is not sent anywhere, and it is shown back to you on your profile page.

Leaderboards currently show profiles on your own device. Your player name, picture and best scores are not published to an online leaderboard. Use a nickname if other people share your browser.

Deleting it. Use the privacy settings page to inspect, download or delete saved browser data. Clearing all of PLAYPILE's site data in your browser also removes local profiles and saves. Keep a save code first if you want to restore your progress later.

Playing online

Nine games can be played online: PUCK BLITZ (air hockey), SUMO SLAM, ROOFTOP DUEL, ARCHER DUEL, HOOPS, EGG WARS, SKY ACES, TOY SOLDIER BLITZ and INKVALE. You join a game in one of three ways: a four-letter room code, a link a friend sends you, or Quick match, which pairs you with whoever else is waiting in the same game, so that player may be a stranger. To connect, both browsers briefly contact a free public matchmaking server run by the PeerJS project, which sees the room code and each browser's IP address. After that the game goes directly between the browsers, which means each player's browser can see the other's IP address, as with any direct video call or online game. Nothing is stored about the game and there is no chat or messaging. To play only with people you know, use a room code instead of Quick match.

Your choices and rights

  • On the privacy settings page you can switch to non-personalised adverts, see everything the site has saved in your browser, download it and delete it.
  • Choosing non-personalised adverts is how you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising under US state laws such as the CCPA.
  • If you are in the UK, the EU or another place with similar laws, you have the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. For a privacy request, email the address below; for adverts, Google's own tools are linked above. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Saved progress and local profiles stay in your browser until you delete them. Save codes you copy elsewhere stay there until you remove those copies.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how often PLAYPILE is visited, which pages are popular and how quickly pages load. Your browser sends page and performance measurements to Cloudflare. Reports include page views, visits, referring websites, country, browser and device type.

Cloudflare's analytics does not use cookies or local storage to identify visitors and does not fingerprint individuals. We do not send profile names, scores, save codes or other saved game data to it. These aggregate statistics are separate from advertising and from the progress kept on your device.

Read more about Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare's privacy policy. Blocking the analytics script does not stop the games working.

Children

PLAYPILE is not directed at children under 13. Games do not require an online account or an email address, and profiles and saved progress stay in the browser. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact the address below to ask for its removal. See also the page for parents and the advertising information above.
