Privacy settings

Your choices about adverts, and everything PLAYPILE has saved in this browser - all in one place, and all of it yours to keep or delete.

Adverts

Adverts are served by Google AdSense. Personalised adverts use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Non-personalised adverts are chosen from the page you are on and your rough location only. Either way the site works the same.

This choice is saved in this browser and applies from the next page you open. Choosing non-personalised adverts is also how you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising under US state privacy laws such as California's. You can make the same choice for every website at Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info.

Visitors in Europe and the UK see Google's consent message on their first visit.

Your saved data

Every game keeps its scores, unlocks and settings in this browser's local storage. Here is all of it. Nothing below is on a server unless you have signed in to a cloud account.

Looking...

A downloaded copy is a plain text file you can keep, or use as a backup. For moving your progress to another device, the save code in the Sign in panel is easier.
