Updates

What has changed on PLAYPILE, newest first. The games are worked on all the time; this is the list of the changes a player would notice.

9 October 2026 — thirteen new games

  • STACK ATTACK — Catch the order on a wobbly bun, in the right order, before the tower falls over.
  • SNOWBALL ROYALE — A snowball fight at dusk: duck behind snow walls, lob over them, and roll a giant one to flatten everybody.
  • WRECKING BALL — One crane, one wrecking ball, 30 seconds: knock the whole block down.
  • MOWER MADNESS — A ride-on mower, a ticking clock, and a lawn full of gnomes that will not get out of the way.
  • SUSHI CONVEYOR — Grab the right plate off three speeding belts and get it to the right face before they storm out.
  • BOMB SQUAD — A ticking bomb, a paper manual and three strikes to spare: snip, flip, read, don't panic.
  • GRAPPLE GOBLIN — Hold to hook, let go to fling: one button, one goblin, and twenty caves full of gold.
  • PARRY — You only block, and every arrow, axe and fireball lands on the beat.
  • ONE TILE — Crack the safe, grab the gold and get out before the guard turns round.
  • DEMOLITION DERBY — Eight cars, one dirt bowl, and the last one still running wins.
  • TOUGE DRIFT — Slide an 80s hatchback down an endless Japanese mountain pass at dusk, one long drift at a time.
  • BLADE DASH — Tap to dash-slash. Every kill refills your dash, so the whole room can be one unbroken chain.
  • TINY HARBOR — Keep a tiny island: tie up the trading boats, light them in at night, and grow a leaky tent into a cottage.

8 October 2026 — privacy settings, and pages for parents

  • A privacy settings page: choose non-personalised adverts, see every score and save the site has kept in your browser, download a copy, or delete it.
  • Every game page now says plainly what is in the game, and has a link for reporting a problem.
  • New pages: terms of use, contact, for parents, accessibility (with a warning about flashing lights) and credits.
  • The privacy policy now explains what online play shares between two players' browsers.

7 October 2026 — INKVALE

  • INKVALE is on the pile: a tower defence painted in ink and watercolour, twelve battles long. Your towers paint the enemy — archers ochre, mages blue, artillery red — and two colours on one foe mix into a hex, a root of vines or a burst of fire. The spells are drawn on the road with the pointer.
  • Every battle pays coins, win or lose, for hiring five more warriors in the Hall.
  • Two Wardens: play it with a friend over the internet with a four-letter room code.
  • A new Strategy category to put it in.

7 October 2026 — a smaller pile, every game played through

  • SNAKE, LINES, RALLY, INVASION, BRICKS, HOPPER and LIGHTS OUT have been taken off. The pile is smaller and every game left on it is one of ours.
  • Every game was played through again by hand, and what that turned up is fixed:
  • HIGHRISE: the arms are the right size — the pistol, rifle and shotgun sit low and to the right instead of filling half the screen, and your other hand is on the shotgun's pump.
  • NIGHT SHIFT: the police no longer start searching before you have done anything; the first units on the scene actually reach it now; the job has an arrow pointing to it; and the panels on the left can be read.
  • CROSSING is pixel art all the way through, menus and scoreboard included. STACK builds into a sky that turns to sunset and night the higher you go. DRIFTER has a new look: glowing rocks, a nebula, and a ship you can see.
  • EXPOSURE, KICKBACK, GRIND CITY, SOUTHPAW and DUNGEON QUEST fit the player on the page instead of running off its edges.
  • SPACE starts the small games from their menus. Smaller fixes in PIPEWORKS, SPIKE, QUICKDRAW and KICKBACK.

7 October 2026 — two new games, and a guide for every one

  • SOUTHPAW is on the pile: arcade pixel boxing against ten opponents, each with a weakness you have to find, plus a gym to spend your winnings in, a locker room, and special modes for wrestling and sumo.
  • PENGUIN DASH is on the pile: a penguin fishing a two-kilometre sea for thirty-six kinds of fish while seals, sharks and orcas hunt it, with deep caverns, twenty-two penguins to unlock and upgrades to buy.
  • Every game page now has a full written guide under the game: how it plays, tips taken from how the game actually works, what is in it, and answers to the usual questions.
  • Categories are real pages now, each with a short introduction and every game in it.
  • Corrected several game descriptions that had fallen behind the games themselves.
  • NIGHT SHIFT: you can now get away after being identified, if you stay unseen long enough; cold plates swap the plates on your car; the burner phone keeps the police from tracing your own.
  • DRIFTER and SPIKE now play properly on a touchscreen. DRIFTER's hyperspace fires once per press instead of draining the tank.
  • SWEEP: the board sizes run smallest to largest, and the clock starts at your first click. SPIKE no longer records two-player rallies as your best.
  • Wrong text fixed in HIGHRISE (thirty-one floors, and the flare gun is on 9), KICKBACK, APEX, QUICKDRAW and PIPEWORKS.
  • No more countdown panel before a game starts, and no advert directly above the game.

23 September 2026 — HOOPS defence, APEX and NASCAR fixes

  • HOOPS: defenders lean on the ball handler instead of reaching through him, and the first seconds of a possession are yours to set up.
  • APEX: the car no longer spins on the spot.
  • NASCAR: every car has its own pit box.

22 September 2026 — NASCAR arrives, APEX grows

  • NASCAR added: twenty ovals, forty cars, the draft, and pit stops.
  • APEX brought up to date with the full game: thirty-three circuits, tyres and pit stops, rain with reflections, damage, and a sky and trees around the tracks.
  • HOOPS: plays in landscape on the site and takes the keyboard properly; you have to leave the floor to block a shot, and a shot in progress can no longer be picked from your hands.
  • KICKBACK: shots land where they should, enemies keep their faces when they turn round, and the guns have a range.
  • HIGHRISE aims at the man you are pointing at. CROSSING is redrawn in pixel art. SPIKE has something to reflect.

20 September 2026 — a new layout, and profiles

  • The site was rebuilt as a portal: a category menu down the side, search, rows of games by category, and a featured game that changes every day.
  • Profiles and save codes: your progress in every game can be copied out as a code and pasted back in on another browser.

17 September 2026 — the new HOOPS

  • HOOPS became three games: half-court streetball, full court with two rings and a clock, and the original stand-still shootout. The other players run plays, stamina matters, coins buy clothes, and you can play a friend online with a four-letter room code.
  • DUNK was taken off, because HOOPS now does everything it did.

14 September 2026 — eight more games

  • Five classics: SNAKE, LINES, RALLY, INVASION and CROSSING.
  • Three big ones: APEX, NIGHT SHIFT and GRIND CITY.
  • CROSSING became endless: the road and river go on for ever and the screen chases you up it.

13 September 2026 — bigger screens

  • R restarts and F goes fullscreen in every quick game.
  • The game area grows with your window instead of stopping at a fixed width.
  • LIGHTS OUT: the torch no longer turns the screen white when you point it at a nearby wall.

12 September 2026 — PLAYPILE goes up

  • The site went live at playpile.net with sixteen games, a privacy policy and a cookie page.
